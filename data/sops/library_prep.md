# Library preparation SOPs (example; replace with your lab's SOPs)

## SOP-LIB-01: Library quantification and loading
Quantify the final adapted library with Qubit dsDNA HS. Convert mass to molarity using the mean fragment size from TapeStation or Femto Pulse. Load 10-20 fmol for gDNA ligation libraries (SQK-LSK114) and 35-50 fmol for amplicons below 1 kb. Do not load by mass alone. If pore occupancy is below 70% in the first hour, top up with library stored on ice, or flush and reload. Library Beads (LIB) must be mixed right before use because they settle quickly.

## SOP-LIB-02: Bead clean-ups and contaminant removal
Use freshly made 80% ethanol, made the same day. Remove all ethanol and air-dry the beads for no more than 30 s, stopping before they crack. Check Nanodrop ratios before library prep: 260/280 about 1.8 and 260/230 at least 2.0. A low 260/230 points to carryover of phenol, guanidine, polysaccharides or ethanol, which block pores and cause fast pore loss with lower Q-scores. For plant or soil extracts, do an extra 0.8x AMPure clean-up or use a polysaccharide-removal column.

## SOP-LIB-03: Post-ligation washes
After adapter ligation, wash with Long Fragment Buffer (LFB) to enrich for fragments over 3 kb, or Short Fragment Buffer (SFB) to keep all fragments. Using SFB on gDNA keeps adapter dimers and short fragments, which raises the fraction of reads under 1 kb. Do not exceed the recommended Ligation Adapter volume.
